Privacy

Your figures stay on your device.

What the editor stores

Live Figure Deck saves your current figure and optional license token in your browser’s local storage. Your equations, labels, and exports are not uploaded to us.

License verification

If you add a Studio license, the token is sent to the Sociobot billing API no more than once per day to confirm that it remains valid. Sociobot and Dodo are the merchant of record and process checkout information under their own policies. This app never receives card details.

Analytics and cookies

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies. The hosting provider may retain short-lived security and access logs.

Your choices

Use “Reset figure” in the editor to remove saved project data. Use “Remove license” to remove the saved token. You can also clear site data in your browser.
